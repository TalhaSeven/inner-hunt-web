Designing for calm and reflection in a mobile app: How we structured the experience in Inner Hunt. 🌿

When building Inner Hunt, the goal wasn't to create another content-heavy feed, but a quiet space that meets you where you are.

Two core interactions shaped the product:

1. "How are you feeling?"
On the Today tab, you can pick from eight states—such as Calm · Focused, Tired · Drained, Anxious · Worried, or Hopeful · Surrendered. The app pairs that state with a fitting Name, and lets you tap "Show another" whenever you want to explore a different perspective.

2. A Consistent Page for Pausing and Reflecting
Every Name follows the same clear anatomy:
01 · Number & vocalized Arabic script (in Tirmidhi's order)
02 · Plain one-sentence meaning & deeper linguistic context
03 · Verses from the Qur'an
04 · A short Reflection to connect the meaning to daily life
05 · Today's Practice — one small, concrete step for the day
06 · Abjad-based dhikr count & one-tap sharing

### EN LinkedIn Post 3 — Engineering & Privacy Philosophy (Zero-Network, Offline-First)
**Images:** Attach `en_04_library_privacy_linkedin_1x1.png` and `en_05_name_of_the_day_linkedin_1x1.png`

```text
Why we built Inner Hunt with zero network requests, no accounts, and no analytics trackers. 🔒

Many apps today ask for an email address before you even see the home screen. For a personal reflection app, that felt wrong.

Here is how we approached the architecture of Inner Hunt:
• 100% Offline-First: All 99 Names, bilingual translations (English & Turkish), Qur'anic verses, and reflections are bundled directly inside the app and validated at build time.
• On-Device Daily Reminders: Daily notifications (defaulting to 21:00) are scheduled locally on the device—no push servers or tokens required.
• Deterministic 99-Day Cycle: Using modular arithmetic over the local day count, the app walks through all 99 Names without repeating a single Name over 99 consecutive days.
• Zero Monetization Friction: Free, no ads, and no subscriptions.

Final testing is underway for iOS and Android. Check out the web companion built with Next.js:
🔗 https://www.innerhunt.com/en

#SoftwareEngineering #PrivacyByDesign #OfflineFirst #Expo #NextJS #InnerHunt
```
